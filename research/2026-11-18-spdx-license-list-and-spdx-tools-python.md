## What it is

The SPDX License List is described as an integral part of the SPDX Specification and a list of commonly found licenses and exceptions used in free and open or collaborative software, data, hardware, or documentation. Its purpose is efficient and reliable identification of licenses and exceptions in SPDX documents, source files, or elsewhere. The tools-python project implements SPDX parsers, converters, validators, and handlers in Python, with support for SPDX v2.2 and v2.3, Tag/Value, RDF, JSON, YAML, and XML, plus experimental SPDX 3.0 support.
